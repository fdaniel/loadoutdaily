Loadout Daily Uploader

Loadout Daily Uploader is a small desktop tool used only by the channel's owner. It uploads the channel's own videos to the Loadout Daily YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API, after the owner has reviewed and approved each one. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
